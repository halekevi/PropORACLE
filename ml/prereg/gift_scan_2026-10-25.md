# Pre-registration: gift scan (Steps 3–5)

**Registered 2026-10-25.** Machine copy: `gift_scan_2026-10-25.json`.
Runner: `py -3.14 -m ml.gift_scan`. Observe / small-stake only.
Nothing auto-promotes. Frozen NFL tracks are not edited.

Written before the first gift is logged.

## Gift

A posted OVER whose floor-table P(clear) is at least **5 points**
above that rung’s breakeven, after a real scraped N-correct is known.

| Rung | Breakeven source | Extra |
|---|---|---|
| Goblin | **per-delta** from the scraped card (GΔ = Standard − Goblin). 3-leg Power at GΔ≈1 is still 1.65x → 84.6%. Deeper GΔ cuts the quote (live_cdp / n_correct_live, plus the card’s goblin_discount_per_unit). A 0.5-line Goblin sitting 3+ under its Standard twin has BE well above 84.6%. No GΔ → not a gift. | line must be ≥1 below L5 min (k≥1). k=0 is fairly priced |
| Standard | scraped 2-pick 3x → 57.7% (3-pick 6x → 55.0%) | k=0 is allowed |
| Demon | scraped rate card for that mix only | no quote → cannot alert |

P(clear) is the floor-persistence cell for sport × prop × role × k.
Unsupported / mapping-fail / n<50 cells are not gifts.

## Adverse selection (before any alert)

The 84% floor is unconditional. PrizePicks may post a Standard / Demon
under a workhorse’s floor because they know minutes, a role change, or
a tag the box scores do not. Every candidate is logged with these
checks, **including fails**. Only a clean pass may alert.

1. **Injury / status** — player is not Q, D, or Out. No fresh Q/D/Out
   on a teammate who shares the role (same-team top-2 on this prop, or
   same position).
2. **Line drop** — if this player-prop-rung’s line fell since the last
   fetch snapshot, flag (possible news). Do not alert as clean.
3. **Confirmed role** — NBA: T−30 starter / minutes snapshot present
   and not bench-or-out. NFL: inactives posted and the player is not
   on that list. Missing confirmation is a flag, not a pass.
4. **Floor margin** — k is on every alert. k=1 is stronger than k=0.

Validity (can we measure): `stat_status` OK, face verified, fresh
board, L5 floor known. Validity fails are skipped, not gifts.

5. **published_after_start** — same per-game lock as the NBA freeze /
   slate-minutes snapshot (`as_of < tip`, T−30 window is still pre-tip).
   Board `fetched_at` / publish time at or after that game’s start is
   tagged `published_after_start=true`. Those rows stay in the raw log
   and **do not enter the pass-vs-flagged measurement pool**. A second
   pass after inactives on a finished game is leakage. Unknown tip
   cannot prove a pre-tip board, so it is excluded from measurement.

## Alerts

Clean gifts only, immediately, to the ntfy topic
(`PROPORACLE_NTFY_TOPIC`). Body: player, prop, line, rung, L5 min, k,
P(clear), breakeven, role.

## Slips

Clean gifts only. 2–3 legs, Flex default (or 2–3 pick Power), different
games, one player per slip, no padding. Mixed rungs allowed. Price the
exact mix at a real scraped quote. Keep only ≥1.4x with +EV at that
quote. Log suggested and any later placed slip with quote + timestamp.
`placed` stays false until a human records it.

## Compare

A weekly rollup of gifts that passed vs gifts that were flagged is
how much of the per-delta Goblin (or Standard) breakeven survives
PrizePicks’ own information. `published_after_start` rows are left
out of that comparison.
